This tool converts multi-line text to a single line?
Yep. This tool takes text containing paragraph breaks, single line breaks, or a combination of both, and compresses it into a single continuous line. Paste your text into the input box and the converted result will appear instantly in the output box below.

What types of plain text line breaks does the tool handle?
The tool processes all common line break formats — single line breaks, double line breaks used to separate paragraphs, and mixed formatting where both types appear in the same body of text. Regardless of the input format, the result is one clean, unbroken line of text.

Will any content be lost during the conversion?
No content is removed from your text. This tool only eliminates the line breaks themselves, and converts the multi-line text into one line. The words and characters in your original text are preserved in full.

Does the tool work with heavily formatted text?
Yes. Whether your text has light formatting with occasional single line breaks or heavy paragraph structuring with multiple blank lines, the tool will flatten it all into a single line regardless of how complex the original formatting is.
